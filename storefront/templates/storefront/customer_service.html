Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding freight timelines, swatch return programs, and artisan care.

How long does delivery take for standard rugs?

In-stock area rugs ship from our Atlanta fulfillment center within 2 to 3 business days via premier freight carriers. Standard transit takes 3 to 7 business days depending on location. For white-glove delivery, the carrier will contact you 24–48 hours ahead to schedule an appointment window.

How does the 18" Swatch credit program work?

18" sample swatches can be ordered directly from each product page for $25. When you decide on a rug for your project, 100% of your swatch investment is credited towards the final area rug purchase. Swatches can also be returned in pristine condition within 30 days for store credit.

What rug pad is recommended for AZI HOME rugs?

We strongly recommend a high-density, dual-surface rug pad (felt with natural rubber backing). A quality pad prevents slipping, protects fine flooring finishes, cushions fiber crush under heavy foot traffic, and prolongs knot integrity for decades.

How do I request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)?

Submit an inquiry through our Contact Portal selecting "Returns, Swatch Exchange & RMA" or email {{ settings.contact_email|default:"info@azi-home.com" }} with your order number. Our concierge will issue an RMA number and prepaid freight return paperwork.
