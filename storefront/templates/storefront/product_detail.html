Vacuuming: Vacuum regularly using suction only. Do not use a rotating beater brush or high power setting, as this can pull delicate wool fibers.

Natural Wool Shedding: Natural wool hand-knotted rugs will shed loose fibers for the first 3–6 months. This is a normal hallmark of pure wool craftsmanship and will diminish naturally.

Spill Cleanup: Blot spills immediately with a clean, un-dyed cloth or paper towel. Never rub vigorously. For set-in stains, use lukewarm water and mild wool-safe detergent.

Professional Cleaning: We recommend professional in-plant immersion rug cleaning every 2–3 years to preserve natural wool lanolin oils.
