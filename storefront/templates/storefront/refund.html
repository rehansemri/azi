1. 30-Day Return Window for Standard Catalog Sizes

We want you and your clients to love every rug chosen. If a standard catalog size (such as 2'X3', 2'6"X8' Runner, 6'X9', 8'X10', 9'X12', or 10'X14') does not harmonize with your lighting or interior scheme, you may return it within thirty (30) calendar days from receipt for a full product refund (less actual outbound freight fees where applicable).

To qualify for a refund, the merchandise must:

  • Be in brand-new, unused condition with original protective tags intact.
  • Free from pet dander, perfume odors, soil, foot traffic stains, or liquid spills.
  • Packaged securely in heavy-duty plastic wrap to prevent transit soil and moisture exposure.

2. Step-by-Step Return Authorization (RMA) Process

Returns cannot be accepted at our fulfillment warehouses without an active RMA number. To initiate a return:

  1. Request RMA: Submit an inquiry via our Contact Portal (selecting "Returns, Swatch Exchange & RMA") or email {{ settings.contact_email|default:"info@azi-home.com" }} with your Order ID and photo of the rug.
  2. Receive Return Paperwork: Our concierge will issue an RMA document and provide discounted prepaid freight Bill of Lading (BOL) shipping labels within 24 business hours.
  3. Package Securely: Roll the rug tightly face-in and double-wrap in durable 4-mil plastic sheeting, sealing all ends and seams with heavy packing tape.
  4. Carrier Hand-Off: Coordinate scheduled pick-up with our designated freight carrier or drop off smaller parcel sizes at an authorized facility.

3. 18" Swatch Sample Credit & Exchange

We encourage ordering 18" swatches prior to finalizing large rug specifications:

  • Automatic 100% Invoice Credit: When you purchase an area rug from that collection, your swatch fee ($25) is fully credited on the rug invoice.
  • Swatch Returns: You may also return loan swatches within thirty (30) days in pristine condition to receive a 100% store merchandise credit for future projects.

4. Damaged or Defective Items in Transit

If your shipment shows external carton tearing or transit damage upon delivery, note the damage on the carrier's delivery receipt (Bill of Lading). Notify AZI HOME within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt with clear photos of the packaging and damage. We will immediately expedite a complimentary replacement and manage carrier claims on your behalf.

5. Non-Returnable Items

The following categories are final sale and non-returnable:

  • Custom or bespoke sizes woven to specific architectural client dimensions.
  • Clearance, sample-sale, or retired discontinued collection items marked "Final Sale".
  • Used rug pads once opened from factory shrink-wrap (due to hygiene protocols).

6. Refund Disbursement

Once received at our Norcross, GA fulfillment hub, our quality inspection team verifies product condition within 48 hours. Approved refunds are credited directly back to your original payment method (or trade account ledger) within 3 to 5 business days. You will receive an automated confirmation email with full credit details.

Our returns concierge is here to assist with packaging advice, Bill of Lading generation, or swatch trade credits.
