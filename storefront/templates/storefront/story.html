In 1978, N.K. Chaudhary founded Azi Home with two looms and nine artisans. Rejecting predatory middlemen, he created an unprecedented grassroots network that directly empowered rural women and hereditary weavers across Bhadohi, India.

Today, AZI HOME continues this legacy under daughter Asha Chaudhary’s leadership in Atlanta, bringing museum-grade handmade luxury to premier interior designers and architectural projects worldwide.
