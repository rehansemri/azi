2. Authentic Handcrafted Artistry & Natural Variation Disclosure

Every AZI HOME rug is an authentic handcrafted work of art. Unlike mass-produced synthetic machine rugs, our collections are woven on hereditary looms by artisan weavers across Rajasthan, India. By placing an order, you acknowledge and accept that:

  • Dimensional Tolerances: Hand-knotting and hand-tufting naturally result in slight size variances of up to +/- 3% from nominal catalog measurements.
  • Yarn Dye Lots: Unrefined organic wool, raw silk, and hand-spun jute absorb natural vegetable and chrome dyes uniquely. Subtle color striations (abrash) and dye-lot variations between production runs are celebrated hallmarks of hand-loomed luxury, not defects.
  • Sprouting & Shedding: High-grade unbleached wool naturally sheds loose surface fibers during initial weeks of use. Loose tufts ("sprouts") should be trimmed flush with sharp scissors, never pulled.

Concealed damage must be formally submitted with high-resolution photography to AZI HOME within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt.
